iPhone: send ALL photos without selecting them

You make a small “opdracht” (shortcut) once in the iPhone app Opdrachten (in English: Shortcuts). It takes about 5 minutes. After that, one tap sends all your photos.

  1. Open the app Opdrachten and tap + (top right).
  2. In the search bar at the bottom type foto and tap Zoek foto's (Find Photos).
  3. Search herhaal and tap Herhaal met elk (Repeat with Each).
  4. Search url and tap Haal inhoud van URL op (Get Contents of URL). It must sit between “Herhaal met elk” and “Einde herhaling”. If it landed below, hold it and drag it up. Then:
    • Tap the blue word URL and paste this (tap Copy first):
    • Tap the small arrow › to show more options. Set Methode to POST.
    • The row below it (the request body) → choose Bestand (File). Tap the new Bestand field and choose Herhaalobject (Repeat Item).
  5. Tap ▶ (bottom right) to start. Tap Sta toe (Allow) when it asks for your photos or the network.

The PC names each photo after the date it was taken and never keeps the same photo twice, so running it again is safe. Keep the iPhone unlocked with the app open while it runs.

8000 photos? Do it in parts: in Zoek foto's tap Voeg filter toe and use the Aanmaakdatum (creation date) filter to send for example one year at a time.

Start the PC program with the same code every time (type it at the start, or use --code). Otherwise the link you pasted stops working.
